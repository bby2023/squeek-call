How a slip settles

You call RUNNING or IDLE. The stake leaves the stack for 12 seconds. At the clock the page fetches GET https://api.squeekchain.com/v1/status with no query string and reads measurement.coreStatus.

ACTIVE and RUNNING count as a RUNNING call. IDLE counts as IDLE. COUNTER_FAULT, a missing measurement, or a failed fetch returns the stake and does not change the streak. The poll that spins the wheel is a different request. It never settles a slip.

Price, before the streak: over the last 12 distinct readings, a side pays 1.1× plus 2.9× the other side's share. Under four readings both sides pay 1.5×. Streak multiplies that price by ×1, ×1.3, ×1.6, ×2, then ×2.5, and the combined rate caps at 6×. The rate freezes on the slip. Returned chips are the stake times the locked rate, rounded. Exact rounding is in rules.js.

A miss resets the streak. Banking the streak keeps the chips and sets the multiplier back to ×1. A fresh book is 1,000 chips, and only opens when the stack is empty. The old slips stay.

Anchor height on a slip is builder.anchor.blockHeight, the settled frontier. Projected blocks are not used. RPM is measurement.rpmMilli.

Chips live in localStorage under squeek-call-v2. They are a score on this machine. The page does not connect a wallet, sign a message, or send a transaction.
