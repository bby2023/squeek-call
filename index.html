      How a round settles
      1. You lock RUNNING or IDLE. Clock starts at 12.
      2. Live panel keeps polling; wheel follows rpmMilli. Useful for vibes, not for the score.
      3. At zero: one more GET /v1/status. measurement.coreStatus is the answer.
      4. Hit/miss scored in the browser. Score + log in localStorage.
